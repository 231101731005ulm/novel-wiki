Kamen Rider Zeztz

Kamen Rider Zeztz (仮面ライダーゼッツ Kamen Raidā Zettsu), or stylized as Kamen Rider ZEZTZ, is a Japanese Tokusatsu drama in Toei Company's Kamen Rider Series[cite: 2]. It is the seventh series to debut in the Reiwa Era and the fortieth overall[cite: 2].

The series premiered on September 7, 2025, joining No.1 Sentai Gozyuger in the Super Hero Time lineup after the finale of Kamen Rider Gavv[cite: 2].

Production

Development

During an early planning meeting with Shin-ichiro Shirakura and Hideaki Tsukada, screenwriter Yuya Takahashi pitched an idea about a dream-themed Rider, which was approved[cite: 2]. However as "dreams" was an extremely vague and broad concept, the concept of "secret agents" was added[cite: 2].

Post-Production

Toei Company confirmed and filed the trademark for Zeztz on March 19, 2025[cite: 2]. On July 25, 2025, the day of the premiere of Kamen Rider Gavv: Invaders of the House of Snacks, actor Ryutaro Imai was announced to have been cast in the lead role of Baku Yorozu[cite: 2].
